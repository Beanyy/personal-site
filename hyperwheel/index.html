Hyperwheel

Personal project · Built in 2021

A counter-rotating LED festival totem. Designed in Fusion 360, 3D printed and wired by me.

STAGE 01

Exploded view

The Fusion 360 model: two lattice wheels, a coaxial shaft, three bevel gears, a motor, a bearing housing and an electronics bay. Every part was 3D printed.

The motor drives one bevel gear meshed between two facing gears, so they spin in opposite directions. Coaxial shafts carry each one to its own wheel.

A custom carrier board for an STM32 Blue Pill, with a BLE module, motor driver, buck converter and two LED channels.
