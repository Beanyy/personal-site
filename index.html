About

(Me)

Software by day. Things that light up by night.

By day I write software that sits right next to the hardware: drivers, GPUs, and now AR glasses. After hours I trade the keyboard for a soldering iron, a 3D printer and whatever else the next build needs.

Every project is an excuse to learn something new, from CAD to circuit boards to firmware. The best part is finishing it and sharing it with people.
